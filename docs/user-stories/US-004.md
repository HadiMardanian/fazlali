## Narrative
As a Photographer/Partner I want official uploads, live slideshow, and branded poster kit so that events get professional delivery and venue promotion.

## Business logic
- Studio scoped token for one Room; official content flagged separate.
- Slideshow: approved-photos stream, configurable delay, logo overlay, enable/disable, QR payload.
- Poster kit: QR, short link, title/date, instructions, branding.
